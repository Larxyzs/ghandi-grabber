    How it works

    1. Pick a brand recipe at the top, or leave it on Auto. Auto looks at the website name and picks the right one.
    2. Find products. Open a brand website (the brand buttons open the Morocco sites) and go to a list, like “Lave-linge”. Press Find all products here. On a single product page, press Grab this product. You can also paste links.
    3. Grab. Press the green button at the bottom. A small window opens for each product, reads it, and closes.
    4. Check. Products with an amber label need a quick look. Click a row to see and fix anything.
    5. Send. Press Download .json. Then give that file to the importer page on your site.

    Leave Chrome open while it works. Everything is saved after every product.
