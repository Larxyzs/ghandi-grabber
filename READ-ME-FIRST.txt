GHANDI PRODUCT GRABBER  (version 3.7)
===================================

What is this?
-------------
A helper that lives inside Chrome. It does 3 jobs:

  1. FIND   - you give it a category page ("All fridges"). It finds every
              product link on it, even behind "Voir plus" and page 2, 3, 4.
  2. GRAB   - it opens each product by itself and reads: the name, the price,
              the main picture, all the other pictures, and ALL the
              specification sections (Caractéristiques, Dimensions, ...).
  3. SEND   - it makes ONE file (.json) with everything.
              You give that file to your website. Your website adds all the
              products for you. (The file LOVABLE-PROMPT.md has the words
              to give to Lovable so it builds that "importer" page.)

Recipes included: Whirlpool, LG, Candy, TCL, Samsung, Haier, Bosch.
Each one knows how its brand's website is built.

HOW SURE ARE WE?
Each brand was studied on its real Morocco website (the text of real pages:
links, buttons, section names, picture addresses). Then a copy of each site
was built and the whole thing (find -> grab -> download) was tested 7 times
in a real Chrome: 7 out of 7 passed, for all 7 brands.

ELECTROPLANET-STYLE SPEC SHEETS + "WHAT ARE YOU GRABBING?" (new in 3.7)
Every brand writes the same facts in its own words (LG: "Réservation marche/arrêt
(24 heures)", Samsung: "Minuterie 24 heures"...). Now every product also gets ONE
clean sheet, with the same sections and lines as Electroplanet, whatever the brand:
  Autre Caractéristique (Marque, Code produit), Caractéristiques, Puissance,
  Poids Et Dimensions, Contenu De L'emballage, Réseau...
  e.g. "Minuteur = Oui", "Capacité de rafraîchissement (nominale) = 24000 BTU/h",
  "Fréquence d'entrée AC = 50 Hz", "Taille de l'écran = 139,7 cm (55")".
Nothing is invented: a line is shown only when the brand page (or the name) says it.
  - Before finding / grabbing, the side tab asks: "What are you grabbing?"
    (Réfrigérateur, Climatiseur, Lave-linge, TV, Table de cuisson, Four encastrable,
    Micro-ondes encastrable, Évier, Lave-vaisselle, Ventilateur, or "Mixed").
    Its guess is already marked. You can turn the question off in "Speed & limits".
  - Change the type of one product in its details, or of many with the
    selection bar ("Type ... Set").
  - In the product details: "Electroplanet style" / "Brand's original" to see both.
  - "Spec sheet" at the top: what goes into the file and to your site.
    The brand's original lines are always kept too (brandSpecGroups in the file).

UPDATES WITHOUT UNINSTALLING (new in 3.6)
Install this version ONE last time, from a folder that stays put
(for example Documents\ghandi-product-grabber), not from Downloads or a zip.
After that, new versions come by themselves:
  - When there is a new version, a blue box appears at the top of the side tab:
    "New version X is ready" with what changed. Nothing changes until you click.
  - Click "Install update". The first time only, Chrome asks you to pick the
    extension folder (the one you loaded in chrome://extensions) and to allow it
    to edit the files. Pick that folder and click "Allow" / "Edit files".
  - It downloads the new files, checks them, keeps a copy of the old ones in
    "backup-previous", and restarts. Your products and settings stay.
  - If the side tab does not come back by itself: open chrome://extensions and
    press the round arrow (reload) on Ghandi Product Grabber.
  - Changed your mind? "Speed & limits" -> "Undo last update".
  - "Speed & limits" -> "Check for updates" looks right now.
Do not delete or move the extension folder, or the next update will ask for it again.

AIR CONDITIONERS & FANS: SLIDESHOW MODE (new in 3.5)
On an air conditioner or fan page (the address or the name says climatiseur,
climatisation, air conditioner, ventilateur, BTU...) the helper reads ONLY:
  - the pictures of the product's slideshow (the first slide = main picture),
  - the lines inside the specifications box.
Nothing from the rest of the page (feature blocks, videos, other products).
Lines that say "N/A" are skipped. These products show "❄ slideshow mode".
You choose how it works in the side tab, under "Air conditioners & fans":
  - Slideshow + specs box        (normal)
  - Slideshow only, no specs
  - Slideshow for every product
  - Off (read the whole page)

NOT ONLY FRIDGES (new in 3.4)
Every recipe was tested on many kinds of products, about 10 per brand
(73 products in total, all passed): TVs, soundbars, monitors, washing
machines, dryers, dishwashers, ovens, hobs, hoods, cookers, microwaves,
air conditioners, air purifiers, vacuums, water heaters, freezers, coffee
machines, kettles, air fryers, kitchen machines...
"Produits similaires", "Produit recommandé", "Vous aimerez peut-être aussi",
"Récemment consultés", "Achetez ces produits également", "À découvrir"...
are all skipped: no pictures, specs or prices of OTHER products.
Real websites can still differ in small ways. Test 3 products per brand the
first time. If something is missing, use "Copy problem report" (Part 5).

The websites used (Morocco):
  Whirlpool  whirlpool.ma          (no prices on the site)
  LG         lg.com/ma             (no prices on the site)
  Candy      candy-home.com/fr_MA  (no prices on the site)

When a site has no price, the price stays empty. Fill it in on your site.


PART 1 - INSTALL IT (one time only, about 3 minutes)
----------------------------------------------------
Think of it like putting a new toy into your toy box.

1. Find the file  ghandi-product-grabber.zip  that you downloaded.
2. Open it so the folder comes out:
     - Windows: right-click the zip, click "Extract All...", then "Extract".
     - Mac: double-click the zip.
3. You now have a folder called  ghandi-product-grabber.
   Put it somewhere it can STAY FOREVER, like Documents.
   (If you delete or move this folder later, the extension stops working.)
4. Open Google Chrome.
5. Click the address bar (where you type website names).
   Type this and press Enter:
        chrome://extensions
6. In the top-right corner, turn ON the switch "Developer mode".
7. Click  "Load unpacked"  (top-left).
8. Click the folder  ghandi-product-grabber  (the one with manifest.json
   inside). Then click "Select Folder".
9. You will see a card: "Ghandi Product Grabber".
10. Click the puzzle-piece icon at the top-right of Chrome.
    Click the PIN next to "Ghandi Product Grabber".

Done! Chrome may say the extension "can read websites". That is normal.
It reads a page only when YOU ask. Nothing is sent anywhere.
Everything stays on your computer.

ALREADY HAD AN OLDER VERSION?  Unzip the new zip, copy everything into your old
folder (say "Replace" for all files), then go to chrome://extensions and
click the round Reload arrow on the card. That is all.


PART 2 - USE IT (the little side tab)
-------------------------------------
Click the green icon on the toolbar. A little tab opens on the RIGHT side of
Chrome (it is called the "side panel"). It stays open while you look at
other pages. It remembers everything.
  - Pull its left edge to make it wider or thinner.
  - The "Full page" button at the top opens the same thing in a big tab.
  - Needs Chrome version 116 or newer (Chrome updates by itself).
In the little tab, the first box ("Where are the products?") folds away
by itself when products appear. Press "Show" to open it again.
Each product is a small card. Tap the card to see and fix everything.

AT THE TOP
  Brand recipe         Leave it on "Auto". It reads the website name and
                       picks the right brand. Or choose a brand yourself.
                       The grey line under it tells you what Auto will use.
  Category on my site  Type the category name you use on your website,
                       for example "Réfrigérateurs". Every product you add
                       gets it. (You can change it later, one by one or
                       for many at once.)
  Speed & limits       How many small windows at once (4 is normal, 6 is
                       fast) and how many pages to follow on a category.
                       "Do not download pictures while reading" is ON: the
                       small windows skip pictures, videos and ad trackers.
                       You still get every picture LINK. This is much faster.
                       If a website starts to fail, use fewer windows.

STEP 1 - WHERE ARE THE PRODUCTS?  (the easy way)
  1. Click a brand button (Whirlpool, LG, Candy ...). Its Morocco website
     opens in a new tab.
  2. On that website, go to a LIST of products, like "Lave-linge".
  3. Look at the side tab: the box "The page you are on" shows the page,
     the brand it found, and if it is a list or a single product.
  4. Press "Find all products here". It clicks "Voir plus" and goes to the
     next pages by itself, and then GRABS them all by itself
     (untick "After finding them, grab them right away" if you only want
     the links). On a single product page, press "Grab this product".
     If the page is already in your list, the box says so (green line).
  The other way: press "or paste a link" and paste a category link or
  product links (one per line).
  Bosch tip: the first Bosch pages show only 4 products and a link
  "Voir tous les produits". The extension follows that link by itself.

EVEN FASTER: RIGHT-CLICK
  On any brand page, right-click and choose:
     "Ghandi: find all products on this page"
     "Ghandi: grab this product"
  On a product LINK, right-click: "Ghandi: grab this product link".
  Select a part of a page with many links, right-click:
     "Ghandi: grab all products in my selection".
  The side tab opens by itself so you can watch.

CATEGORY
  Leave "Category on my site" EMPTY and each product gets the name of the
  list page it came from (for example "Lave-linge pose libre").
  Or type your own. Names you used before are suggested as you type.

STEP 2 - PRODUCTS (the list on the right)
  Every product is a row with its picture, name, brand, price and a label:
     Not grabbed   I have only the link so far.
     In line / Working   It is being read right now.
     Done          All good.
     Check me      I read it, but something may be missing. Click the row
                   to see what. (Example: no picture found.)
     Failed        The page could not be read. Press the round arrow to try
                   again. I also try twice by myself.
  Click a row to open the details. You can FIX anything there (name, model,
  price, pictures, every specification line) and press "Save changes".
  The buttons at the top of the list (All / Done / Check me / ...) filter
  the list. The box on the right searches.
  Tick the little boxes to select many rows. A yellow bar appears:
  grab them, try again, set their category, or remove them.

THE DARK BAR AT THE BOTTOM (the control panel)
  The little lights: ONE LIGHT PER PRODUCT.
     green = done, yellow = check me, red = failed, blue = working now.
  Big green "Grab"   Starts reading products. If you ticked some rows it
                     reads those; if not, it reads all that are "Not grabbed".
  "Stop"             Stops after the products being read right now.
  "Try the failed ones again"  appears when some products failed.
  "Send to my site"  Choose what goes in the file. The first choice,
                     "New since my last download", gives you only the
                     products you have NOT downloaded yet. So you can grab
                     fridges today, washing machines tomorrow, and each file
                     has only the new ones. Then:
     "Download .json"  saves the file on your computer.
     "Copy"            copies the same text so you can paste it.

IMPORTANT
  - Keep Chrome open while it works. You can use other tabs.
  - The green icon shows how many products are left. At the end it shows
    a green tick (or a red number if some failed). For long jobs, a small
    message pops up on your computer when it is finished.
  - In the details window (click a product): click a picture to make it
    the MAIN picture, click x to remove a picture. Little "copy" buttons
    copy the name, the picture links, or the specifications as text
    (handy to paste into your admin page by hand).
  - A page that no longer exists (404) is marked Failed at once.
  - Do NOT close the small windows yourself.
  - Everything is saved after every product. If Chrome closes, open the
    dashboard again and press "Grab" again: it continues where it stopped.
  - QUICK READ (new, the fastest way): for most products the extension does
    not open any window at all. It downloads the page as plain text and reads
    it in a fraction of a second (like a robot reading the page's source).
    Whirlpool, Candy and Haier pages work like this; LG and Samsung too when
    their page text has everything. If anything is missing (a part that only
    appears after a click, pictures added later), it automatically uses the
    small window instead, so the result is never worse.
    Rows read this way show "⚡ quick read". You can switch it off in
    "Speed & limits" if a brand ever gives strange results.
    Category pages are read the same way (no window) when possible.
  - Faster than before: each little window is reused from product to
    product, and it stops waiting as soon as everything is read. On real
    websites expect about 2 to 6 seconds per product with 4 windows
    (it depends on how fast the brand's website and your internet are).
    The bottom bar shows the speed and the time left while it works.
  - Always check the PRICE on the first products of each brand.
    Shops can show an old price, a sale price and extra fees.


PART 3A - SEND STRAIGHT TO YOUR SITE (no file)
---------------------------------------------
One time: follow "What YOU do" in LOVABLE-PROMPT-CONNECT.md.
Then: in the side tab click "My site", paste the address and the key from
your site's admin page "Extension Chrome", click "Save and test".
Every day:
  1. Grab products. Click "Select all" (or tick some rows).
  2. Click "Import to site" (bottom). Choose which products, type a section
     to suggest if you want, click "Send".
  3. On your site's admin a notification appears:
     "Annuler" = wipe them.  "Ajouter au site" = choose products and the
     section for each, then add them.
Rows that were sent show "✓ sent to site".


PART 3 - GIVE THE FILE TO YOUR WEBSITE
--------------------------------------
1. Open the file LOVABLE-PROMPT.md (in this folder) with Notepad.
2. Copy the text inside the box and paste it in Lovable's chat.
   Lovable builds a page on your site:  /admin/import
3. On that page, drop the .json file. It shows a preview first.
   Press "Import". Done.
Start with 3 or 4 products to see if it looks right.


PART 4 - ONE HAPPY EXAMPLE
--------------------------
 1. Brand recipe: Auto.   Category on my site: Réfrigérateurs
 2. Paste the LG "combined fridges" page link, press "Find products".
 3. 30 products appear. Press "Grab".
 4. Go for a coffee.
 5. Click the yellow "Check me" labels, fix what is missing.
 6. Press "Download .json". Give it to your website.


PART 5 - IF SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG
---------------------------------
For one product:
  Click its row. At the bottom of the window press "Copy problem report".
For a category page that finds no products (or too few):
  Left box, under the message: press "Copy list report".
Paste the report in the chat with Claude and say what is wrong
("the price is wrong", "pictures missing", "it only found 12 of 40").
The report tells Claude exactly what the page looked like.


PART 6 - AFTER YOU CHANGE ANY FILE (or get a new zip from Claude)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
New zip: unzip it, copy everything into your old folder ("Replace" all).
Then:
1. Go to  chrome://extensions
2. Find "Ghandi Product Grabber".
3. Click the little round arrow (Reload).
4. Close the side tab and click the green icon again.
Nothing else needed. Your list of products is kept.
